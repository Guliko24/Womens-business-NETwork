# Essex Women in Business: Community, Directory & Opportunities Survey

### Survey Introduction
> **Title**: Essex Women in Business: Community, Directory & Opportunities Survey  
> **Subtitle**: Shaping our local business directory, mentorship programme, and community network.  
> 
> **Intro Text**:  
> Welcome! We are building a dedicated platform to connect, champion, and unite female founders, business owners, and professionals across the Essex region. 
> 
> Beyond local meetups and practical knowledge-sharing workshops, the platform will feature:
> 1. **A curated Essex Female Business & Services Directory** to showcase your business and help locals buy from female-led enterprises.
> 2. **A 1-on-1 and peer Mentorship Programme** to help you grow with confidence.
> 3. **A local Job & Apprenticeship Board** to foster opportunities and nurture emerging talent.
> 
> Your feedback will directly shape our launch events, platform features, and founding directory listings. This survey takes about **3–4 minutes** to complete.

---
